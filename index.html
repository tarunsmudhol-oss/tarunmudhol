01 / VENTURE

Yukshya

A study assistant that lives inside WhatsApp

Students at tier-2 and tier-3 Indian colleges get very little academic help outside class hours, and most ed-tech assumes an app install, a good data plan, and comfort with an unfamiliar interface. That filters out the students who need it most.

Yukshya answers coursework questions over WhatsApp — no download, no new account, works on a low-end handset. Backend is Flask with rate limiting and SSE streaming; documents are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM; the model layer runs Gemini 1.5 Flash with an OpenRouter fallback so a single provider outage doesn't take the bot down. The whole production stack is chosen to cost nothing until there's revenue.

Honest status: the bot is substantially built and not yet deployed. Zero users means zero validation — right now this is a working prototype and a hypothesis, not a proven product. Next milestone is a live pilot on one campus.

03 / APPLIED ML

Face recognition attendance

Roll-call for a full lecture hall, done from one camera frame. Faces are encoded to 512-dimensional embeddings with InsightFace Buffalo-L and matched against an enrolled set using a FAISS index, so lookup stays fast as the roster grows instead of degrading linearly. I worked through the recognition pipeline and wrote the phased study plan the team followed.
